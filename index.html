XBOX 360 / 2009

ForzaMotorsport 3

Released in 2009, Forza Motorsport 3 was the first racing game that truly allowed me to experience a sense of speed and thrill through a screen. This game really had it all, including a great selection of cars ranging from classic JDM to modern LMP cars, including those listed below, as well as a soundtrack that would bring you to another dimension as you were flying down the back straight at Le Mans or drifting through the winding roads of Fujimi Kaido in Japan.

The Nordschleife.

Some tracks let you learn a corner every minute or so. Miss one here and it’s a long way around before you get another shot.

Nordschleife is one of the most fun tracks that I have ever raced on. It's good that I can experience each turn without having to actually risk my theoretical million-dollar hypercar. This track contains every kind of turn, making it the perfect stomping ground for a beast yet to be tamed.
